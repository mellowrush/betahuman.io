About
The builder

Senior frontend developer. Self-taught. Background in creative and advertising work. Based in Brussels. Currently rebuilding — not from wreckage, but from the quiet realisation that the life you end up with isn't always the one you consciously chose.

What I do

Build interfaces with JavaScript and React. Care about the gap between functional and beautiful. Treat low complexity as a feature, not a limitation.

How I think

In frameworks and reference points. I anchor new ideas to exemplars, reverse-engineer the principles, then generate variations. First principles over trends, always.

What I reject

Generic advice. Corporate nonsense. Empty authority. Performative expertise. Trendy content that forgets to be useful.

What this version is

v0.1. A working title. The beta is real, not ironic. This site will change as I do. That's the point.
